Things to do in Playa del Carmen

Playa del Carmen is the sweet spot between Cancún and Tulum: walkable beaches, Quinta Avenida lined with restaurants and a 30-min ferry to Cozumel to dive with turtles.

📅 Suggested days: 3–5

Things to do in Playa del Carmen

Quinta Avenida

A 4-km pedestrian street of shops, bars and restaurants; the social heart of the city.

Cozumel by ferry

30-min ferry; snorkel or dive the world's second-largest reef.

Nearby cenotes

Cristalino, Azul and Jardín del Edén 20 min by car; less touristy than the Tulum ones.

Xcaret or Xplor

Eco-archaeological parks 15 min away; full day with evening shows.

Best time to go. November to April: dry weather, calm sea and little sargassum. Avoid September for hurricane risk.
Local tip 🧭 Stay north of Quinta (Calle 34 and up): same beaches, less noise and 30% lower prices than the center.
