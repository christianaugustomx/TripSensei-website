Things to do in Los Angeles

Los Angeles is huge and lived in by car: beaches, Hollywood, mountains and theme parks spread across miles of city.

📅 Suggested days: 4–5

Things to do in Los Angeles

Hollywood Sign & Griffith

Griffith Observatory has the best free views of the sign and the city.

Santa Monica & Venice

The pier, the beach and Venice boardwalk; rent a bike along the coast.

Getty Center

Art and gardens overlooking the ocean; free entry, you only pay parking.

Theme parks

Universal Studios in the city; Disneyland 1h away in Anaheim.

Best time to go. March to May and September to November: clear skies and fewer crowds than summer.
Local tip 🧭 Plan your day by geographic zones: crossing LA at rush hour can take 2 hours. Traffic apps are your best friend.
