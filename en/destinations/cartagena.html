Things to do in Cartagena

Cartagena is the colonial jewel of the Colombian Caribbean: 16th-century walls, color-drenched streets and island beaches a boat ride away.

📅 Suggested days: 3–4

Things to do in Cartagena

Walled city

Get lost among bougainvillea balconies; walk the wall at sunset.

Rosario Islands

Turquoise water 45 min by boat; pick a day pass with lunch included.

Getsemaní

The street-art and nightlife neighborhood; Plaza de la Trinidad after dark.

San Felipe Castle

Colonial fortress with tunnels and city-wide views.

Best time to go. December to April is the dry season. It's hot year-round, so pack light clothing always.
