Things to do in Cusco

Cusco was the Inca empire's capital and the base for Machu Picchu and the Sacred Valley. It sits at 3,400m, so the altitude is real.

📅 Suggested days: 4–6

Things to do in Cusco

Machu Picchu

Train to Aguas Calientes + bus; buy the timed-entry ticket in advance.

Sacred Valley

Pisac, Ollantaytambo and Maras–Moray in a guided day trip.

Rainbow Mountain

A tough high-altitude hike (5,000m); acclimatize in Cusco first.

San Blas & San Pedro market

Artisan quarter and the market to try fruit and fresh juices.

Best time to go. May to September is the dry season, ideal for Machu Picchu. Book tickets and trains weeks ahead.
Local tip 🧭 Arrive a day or two early to acclimatize, drink coca tea, and skip alcohol your first night.
