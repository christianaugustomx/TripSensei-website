Things to do in Medellín

Medellín is Colombia's city of transformation: tech, street art in Comuna 13, Latin America's most comfortable climate and a booming food scene in El Poblado.

📅 Suggested days: 3–5

Things to do in Medellín

Comuna 13

Street art, outdoor escalators and the city's most powerful story; tour with a local guide.

El Poblado & Provenza

The neighborhood with specialty coffee, restaurants and nightlife.

Guatapé & El Peñol rock

2h away: climb 740 steps for a postcard view of the reservoirs.

Metrocable to Arví

Urban cable car + ecological park; use the Cívica card to pay.

Best time to go. December to March and July-August are best: less rain. Temperatures stay 'eternal spring' year-round (65–77°F).
Local tip 🧭 Take the Comuna 13 tour with a local guide (not on your own): the stories behind the murals are what's actually worth it, and locals keep the tour money.
