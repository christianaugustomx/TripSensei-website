3-day Cancún itinerary: the Mexican Caribbean essentials

A short, focused plan for the best of Cancún: beach, a cenote and a day in Isla Mujeres. No time wasted on Chichén Itzá (3h away) or long transfers.

📅 3 days 📖 Cancún travel guide →

Day-by-day plan

Day 1 — Arrival & Hotel Zone

Morning

Land, transfer to your hotel and eat light; guacamole with corn tortillas to kick things off.

Afternoon

Walk Playa Delfines (the best public beach) and swim in the turquoise Caribbean.

Evening

Dinner Downtown (Parque de las Palapas) — cheaper and more authentic than the Hotel Zone.

Day 2 — Tulum + cenote

Morning

Leave at 7 am for Tulum; visit the seaside Maya ruins before 10.

Afternoon

On the way back, stop at Gran Cenote or Dos Ojos to swim and snorkel.

Evening

Back to Cancún and unwind with an oceanfront dinner.

Day 3 — Isla Mujeres

Morning

Ferry from Puerto Juárez (20 min); rent a golf cart for the day.

Afternoon

Playa Norte (one of the Caribbean's best), ceviche by the water and a hammock nap.

Evening

Back to the mainland, early dinner and pack.

Pro tip 🧭 Stay near Puerto Juárez (not the Hotel Zone) if Isla Mujeres is on the plan: the ferry leaves from there and saves you 40 min in transfers.
