7-day Mexico City itinerary: with Puebla and Tepoztlán

A full week in CDMX covers Puebla, Tepoztlán and a Mexican cooking class. All reachable by bus or day tour.

📅 7 days 📖 Mexico City travel guide →

Day-by-day plan

Day 1 — Historic Center

Morning

Zócalo, Cathedral and Templo Mayor.

Afternoon

Palacio Nacional and traditional lunch.

Evening

Mezcal in the Centro.

Day 2 — Coyoacán + Frida

Morning

Casa Azul (with reservation).

Afternoon

Coyoacán market and plaza.

Evening

Dinner in Roma-Condesa.

Day 3 — Teotihuacán

Morning

Balloon ride or early arrival at the pyramids.

Afternoon

Pyramid of the Sun and the Moon.

Evening

Back to CDMX, light dinner.

Day 4 — Xochimilco + Chapultepec

Morning

Trajinera in Xochimilco.

Afternoon

National Museum of Anthropology.

Evening

Dinner in Condesa.

Day 5 — Puebla (full day)

Morning

ETN/ADO bus to Puebla (2h); colonial center and cathedral.

Afternoon

Mole poblano at El Mural; talavera and the artists' quarter.

Evening

Back to CDMX at night.

Day 6 — Tepoztlán

Morning

Drive to Tepoztlán (1.5h), hike the Tepozteco (moderate 2h climb).

Afternoon

Tepoztlán market and traditional food.

Evening

Back to CDMX, quiet dinner.

Day 7 — Cooking + shopping

Morning

Mexican cooking class in San Ángel or Roma.

Afternoon

Mercado de la Ciudadela for crafts.

Evening

Last dinner with margaritas and pack.

Pro tip 🧭 Take the ETN or ADO bus to Puebla — comfortable, Wi-Fi included, leaving every 30 min from TAPO. Way cheaper than a private tour.
