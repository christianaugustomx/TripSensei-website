7-day Medellín itinerary: with Cartagena and the coffee triangle

A week lets you pair Medellín with Cartagena (1h flight) plus two nights at a coffee farm. Two cultures, two climates, one party.

📅 7 days 📖 Medellín travel guide →

Day-by-day plan

Day 1 — Arrival + El Poblado

Morning

Arrive, check in and coffee.

Afternoon

Walk Provenza.

Evening

Dinner at Carmen.

Day 2 — Comuna 13 & downtown

Morning

Comuna 13 tour.

Afternoon

Plaza Botero and Museo de Antioquia.

Evening

Bandeja paisa.

Day 3 — Guatapé

Morning

Climb El Peñol at sunrise.

Afternoon

Painted houses and lunch on the waterfront.

Evening

Back to Medellín.

Day 4 — Coffee farm (overnight)

Morning

Transfer to the farm (2h); tour of the fields.

Afternoon

Cupping with the grower.

Evening

Overnight at the farm; home-cooked dinner.

Day 5 — Coffee fields and return

Morning

Sunrise walk through the fields.

Afternoon

Back to Medellín; nap and coffee in El Poblado.

Evening

Quiet dinner.

Day 6 — Flight to Cartagena

Morning

Flight to Cartagena (1h); check in inside the walled city.

Afternoon

Walk the walls at sunset.

Evening

Dinner in Getsemaní; bar at Plaza Trinidad.

Day 7 — Rosario Islands or return

Morning

Boat to Rosario Islands (half day) if time allows.

Afternoon

Last meal in Cartagena.

Evening

Flight home from Cartagena (more international connections).

Pro tip 🧭 If you visit Cartagena, fly home from there: more international connections than Medellín's airport and you skip the overland return.
