7-day Tulum itinerary: the full Riviera Maya

A week in Tulum lets you reach Bacalar and Holbox without losing beach time. Perfect if you want to see beyond the Instagram postcard.

📅 7 days 📖 Tulum travel guide →

Day-by-day plan

Day 1 — Arrival + beach

Morning

Arrive, check in and coffee in Tulum town.

Afternoon

Bike the beach strip, snack at a beach club.

Evening

Boho dinner facing the sea.

Day 2 — Ruins + Sian Ka'an

Morning

Tulum ruins at opening.

Afternoon

Half-day tour of Sian Ka'an.

Evening

Dinner in town.

Day 3 — Cenote route

Morning

Gran Cenote and Cenote Calavera.

Afternoon

Cenote Dos Ojos for snorkeling.

Evening

Tacos and rest.

Day 4 — Cobá + Akumal

Morning

Cobá pyramid early.

Afternoon

Swim with turtles in Akumal.

Evening

Dinner in town.

Day 5 — Bacalar

Morning

Early drive to Bacalar (3h); sail the seven-color lagoon.

Afternoon

Cenote Azul to swim.

Evening

Overnight in Bacalar or back to Tulum.

Day 6 — Holbox (optional) or slow beach

Morning

Ferry from Chiquilá to Holbox (no cars on the island).

Afternoon

Punta Mosquito beach and a hammock nap.

Evening

Seafood and sunset.

Day 7 — Yoga + return

Morning

Yoga or relaxed beach.

Afternoon

Last meal in town.

Evening

Transfer to Cancún for the flight.

Pro tip 🧭 No cars in Holbox: leave yours in Chiquilá (private parking) and take the ferry. You can cross the island on foot in 1h.
