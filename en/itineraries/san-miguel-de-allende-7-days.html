7-day San Miguel de Allende itinerary: the full Bajío

A week covers San Miguel, Guanajuato, Querétaro and the wine route. Combines colonial, food and wine in one trip.

📅 7 days 📖 San Miguel de Allende travel guide →

Day-by-day plan

Day 1 — Centro and rooftops

Morning

Arrive and coffee at Lavanda.

Afternoon

Jardín Principal and Parroquia.

Evening

Sunset at La Azotea.

Day 2 — Art and markets

Morning

Fábrica La Aurora.

Afternoon

Mercado de Artesanías.

Evening

Dinner at Aperi.

Day 3 — Guanajuato

Morning

Bus to Guanajuato; alleys and Teatro Juárez.

Afternoon

El Pípila and lunch at San Fernando.

Evening

Back to San Miguel.

Day 4 — La Gruta and Atotonilco

Morning

Hot springs early.

Afternoon

Atotonilco Sanctuary.

Evening

Local dinner.

Day 5 — Querétaro wine route

Morning

Drive to Bernal (40 min); climb La Peña.

Afternoon

Tasting at Freixenet or Viñedos La Redonda.

Evening

Back to San Miguel.

Day 6 — Querétaro centro

Morning

Querétaro historic center (1h); aqueduct.

Afternoon

Lunch and Cerrito neighborhood.

Evening

Back to San Miguel.

Day 7 — Cooking class and farewell

Morning

Cooking class or a spa massage.

Afternoon

Last shopping; coffee at Lavanda.

Evening

Farewell dinner.

Pro tip 🧭 For the wine route, leave early to taste around noon: wineries close their kitchens by 4-5 pm and dinner in Bernal before the drive back isn't a great idea.
